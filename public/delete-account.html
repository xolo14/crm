Delete Your Account

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Syncpedia CRM account and the data associated with it, for users of the Syncpedia CRM Android app and web app.

How to Request Deletion

Account deletion is currently handled by our support team to make sure your organization's shared CRM data (leads, records belonging to your team) is handled correctly. To request deletion of your account:

  1. Email support@syncpedia.in from the email address registered to your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Syncpedia CRM account.
  3. We will verify your request and confirm back to you once it is processed.

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days of verification.

What Gets Deleted

DataDeleted?
Account profile (name, email, login credentials)Deleted
Your personal device data synced to the app (contacts matches, location history you generated)Deleted
Call recordings and call logs you createdDeleted, unless your organization's administrator needs them retained as part of your organization's own business/CRM records
Leads, deals, and customer records you worked onRetained by your organization's CRM account, since this data belongs to your employer/organization, not to you individually — your personal account access to it is removed

Retention for Legal/Business Reasons

Some information may be retained for a limited period after deletion where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or preserve records your organization is required to keep (e.g. financial or compliance records).
