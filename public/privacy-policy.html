Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Syncpedia CRM Android application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use it. The App is built for sales and support teams to manage leads, calls, and customer communication on behalf of the organization that has deployed it. By using the App you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

When you sign in, we collect your email address and basic profile details, either entered directly or provided by Google when you use "Sign in with Google". We use this to identify your account and associate it with your organization's CRM data.

1.2 Contacts

With your permission, the App reads your device's contact list so you can place calls and match incoming/outgoing calls to CRM leads. Contact data is used on-device and sent to our servers only where needed to link calls to the correct lead or customer record.

1.3 Call data and call recording

The App can act as your phone's dialer/in-call handler. With your permission, it collects:

  • Call metadata — phone number, call direction, duration, start/end time.
  • Call audio recordings, where recording is enabled for your organization and you have consented to the App's call-handling terms.

Call recording notice: Call recording is intended for legitimate business purposes (quality, training, and CRM record-keeping) on behalf of the organization operating this App. Where required by law, the person recording a call is responsible for providing any necessary notice or obtaining consent from the other party before or during the call. Recordings are uploaded securely to our servers and are accessible only to authorized users of your organization's CRM account.

1.4 Phone state

The App reads phone state (e.g. whether a call is active, the number on an incoming call) to support call handling, show caller information from your CRM, and enable call-recording and in-call features. This data is not shared outside the call-handling and CRM-sync functionality described here.

1.5 Location

With your permission, the App may collect approximate or precise device location to attach location context to field-visit or sales-activity records in the CRM, where that feature is used by your organization.

1.6 Notifications and app usage

We use push notifications to alert you to CRM events (e.g. new leads, reminders). We may collect basic device and diagnostic information (device model, OS version, app version, crash logs) to maintain and improve the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate core CRM features: leads, calls, call recordings, contacts, and notifications.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To sync your activity with your organization's CRM account on our servers.
  • To diagnose problems and improve the App's reliability and performance.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use call recordings, contacts, or location data for advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data collected by the App is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections to our servers and stored in our database. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures — including authenticated API access and certificate-pinned network connections — to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain call records, recordings, and other CRM data for as long as your organization's account is active, or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Your organization's administrator can request deletion of specific records through the CRM.

6. Your Choices

  • You can decline permissions (contacts, phone, microphone, location) at any time in your device settings; some features of the App may not work without them.
  • You can decline the in-app consent screen for call handling/recording, in which case those features are disabled for your account.
  • You can ask your organization's administrator to review, correct, or delete your data, or contact us directly using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use by employees and representatives of organizations, and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective date" above when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
